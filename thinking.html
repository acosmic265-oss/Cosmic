FEATURED ESSAY / 001

Who Am I When Everything Changes?

We often think of identity as something permanent. A name, a face, a collection of memories and experiences.

But everything around us changes. Our opinions change. Our relationships change. Even the person we were yesterday may not be the person we are today.

"If everything about me changes, what exactly remains?"

Maybe identity is not about remaining exactly the same. Maybe it is about the invisible thread connecting every version of ourselves.

This is not a final answer. It is simply a question worth carrying.
