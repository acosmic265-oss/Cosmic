ABOUT COSMIC.

A personal space built for ideas, questions, words and imagination.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE ARCHIVE

I wanted a place for my ideas to stay.

COSMIC. is my personal corner of the internet. It is a place where I can collect the things I research, the poems I write, the questions I think about and the stories I create.

Some of these ideas may become complete works. Some may remain unfinished. I want both to have a place here.

"Ideas become memories when we give them somewhere to exist."
